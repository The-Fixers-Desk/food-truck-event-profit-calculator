# Event Analysis workspace manual review

Use an isolated application-data root and open scenarios with profitable,
break-even and loss outcomes.

- Confirm business profit, target status, break-even customers and warnings
  communicate the outcome immediately without opening calculation details.
- Change demand, revenue, food, labor, fee and profit-target assumptions rapidly;
  confirm every visible dependent metric, warning and decision statement updates.
- Use Save, Save as new, Reset changes, Rename and Compare from the consolidated
  Scenario action region. Verify dirty-navigation confirmation still works.
- Open and close every calculation section with mouse and keyboard. Confirm the
  primary decision metrics remain visible and details retain exact values.
- Test long Event and Scenario names, several labor rows, additional costs and a
  long warning list for wrapping, density and minimal unnecessary scrolling.
- Review widescreen desktop and narrow layouts. Confirm decision summary appears
  first, assumptions second and detailed calculations last without horizontal
  page scrolling or obscured focus.
- Tab through assumption groups, Scenario actions, warnings and calculation
  disclosures. Confirm visible focus and logical source order.
